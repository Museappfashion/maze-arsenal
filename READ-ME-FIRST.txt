MIST MAZE — PLAYER INBOX AND TOP 10 LEADERBOARDS
9 October 2026

Apply this update to your existing game (including the October 7 fixes).

1. Replace/add only the included files at the matching paths in GitHub.
   Keep all other files. Merge folder contents; do not replace whole folders.
2. Deploy the updated commit in Vercel and reload the game.
3. If the previous developer-letters.sql ran successfully, no SQL change is
   needed. If it failed, run the complete included supabase/developer-letters.sql
   in Supabase SQL Editor. The last line is commit; with nothing after it.
   No leaderboard SQL migration or new environment keys are needed for this fix.

PLAYER REPLIES
The main-menu paper/pencil button now says INBOX and shows the number of
developer replies. Open it to read the replies at the TOP of the panel.
Your original letter is expandable underneath; the writing form is below.
While the menu is visible, replies refresh every 30 seconds, when the tab
regains focus, and when the inbox opens. REFRESH also checks immediately.
Players must use the same browser profile that sent the letter; replies are
private to their saved anonymous sign-in. The inbox, leaderboard and analytics
now share one sign-in operation and one client instead of racing to create
separate anonymous users at startup.

LEADERBOARDS
Fixed the code that cut every list to its first entry. Every eligible maze
now retains/displays its top 10, with separate 2D and 3D lists. A player's
personal rank below the list is still shown even when outside the top 10.
Fewer than 10 recorded scores will leave unfilled places blank. A new personal
best no longer replaces the other players' entries. Saved caches keep all 10.
Older locally discarded entries cannot be recovered from that cache, but
global records reload from Supabase on the next successful connection.
